About This Page
Welcome to the BBDU PYQ Hub — a student-made platform created to make exam preparation easier for students of Babu Banarasi Das University (BBDU), Lucknow.
Finding previous year question papers can often be difficult and time-consuming. This website brings Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs) together in one simple and organized place, so students can spend less time searching and more time preparing.

What You Will Find Here
📚 Previous Year Question Papers
📝 Subject-wise question papers
🎓 Course and semester-wise resources
🔍 Easy-to-use search and navigation
📥 Quick access to papers for exam preparation

Why We Created This
We built this platform with one simple goal: to help BBDU students prepare smarter.
PYQs can help you understand the exam pattern, identify frequently asked topics, and practice questions before your exams. This website is designed to make those resources easier for students to find and use.
This is an independent student project and is not officially affispanated with or endorsed by Babu Banarasi Das University.
